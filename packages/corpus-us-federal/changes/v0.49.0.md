# Corpus v0.49.0

## What's new

- Passive activities by activity, 2025–2027 (§ 469, Form 8582), computed inside the return:
  - **Inputs:** `passiveActivities` (0–8), then each activity's `Kind` (`rental_active`, `rental`, `business`, `business_sp`, `ptp`), `Income`, `Loss`, `PriorUnallowed`, `DispositionGain` / `DispositionLoss`, `Disposition` and `Qbi`.
  - **Form 8582:**
    - Passive income absorbs passive losses.
    - The $25,000 special allowance phases out from $100,000 of modified AGI ($12,500 / $50,000 married filing separately living apart; none living together). Modified AGI is AGI without the Form 8582 activities, less taxable Social Security, plus the IRA, student loan and ½-SE deductions.
    - The disallowed loss is spread over the loss activities in proportion (Parts VI–VIII; Reg. § 1.469-1T(f)(2)).
    - Each activity's carryforward is `us.federal.passive.activityN.unallowed`.
  - **Released losses and special cases:**
    - An entire taxable disposition at an overall loss frees every loss (§ 469(g)).
    - PTPs net alone (§ 469(k)).
    - Significant participation income over those losses is nonpassive.
  - **Flows:**
    - Gross income and § 86 income.
    - Net investment income: released losses are deductions, and recharacterized significant participation income is out.
    - QBI, not counted as active for the § 199A(i) minimum.
    - § 461(l) and the EITC § 32(i)(2)(D) test.
    - The kiddie tax refuses a child with passive income.
  - **`compute_return`** shows `forms['8582']`.
  - **Refused:** former passive activities, installment, gift, death and related-party dispositions, Form 8582 Part IX splits, and a QBI-flagged PTP.

Returns without passive activities compute exactly as in v0.48.0. A differential on every existing fixture, the IRS ATS TY2025 scenarios and every pre-existing rule on the regression cases found no difference. An independent rebuild of Form 8582 from the IRS documents agrees on 550 random multi-activity returns.
