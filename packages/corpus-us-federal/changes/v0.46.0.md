# Corpus v0.46.0

## What's new

- Form 2210, the estimated tax penalty (§ 6654), for 2025, 2026 and 2027: `composeForm2210` in compose 0.2.0, `opentax form2210`, MCP `compute_form_2210`.
  - Part I gives the required annual payment (90% / 100% / 110%, the $1,000 tests, no prior-year return).
  - Part III runs Section A and the penalty worksheet. Every payment, including withholding treated as paid on each due date, pays the earliest open underpayment. Box D (actual withholding dates) is used when it's lower. It handles § 7503 weekend due dates and § 6654(h).
  - Rates are each quarter's § 6621 rate: 7% for 2025 Q2 through 2026 Q1, 6% for 2026 Q2, 7% for 2026 Q3 and Q4. A penalty reaching 2027 Q1 or later refuses until the IRS announces that rate.
- Schedule AI for every income type the engine models. Each column is the whole return computed on the period's cumulative amounts × 4, 2.4, 1.5 and 1, with the standard deduction in full, and the lower penalty wins (box C).
  - It refuses, with the reason, for: the foreign earned income exclusion, prior-year carryovers, the premium tax credit, Form 8615, Schedule H, a dependent filer, a SALT cap binding in a period, or a period with self-employment net earnings under $400.
- Projection for tax years 2028–2035 at an inflation rate you choose: `getProjectedFederalCorpus({ year, inflationPercent, wageGrowthPercent })`, `--project-inflation`, MCP `projection`.
  - Every indexed amount is carried forward from 2027 by its own statutory formula and rounding. The starting point is the IRS's own 2027 index (C-CPI-U as first published; an 11-month average because October 2025 was never published). The wage base and household threshold use the wage index.
  - Known law is applied: SALT 101% through 2029, then $10,000 from 2030; the tips, overtime, car-loan and senior deductions end after 2028.
  - The premium tax credit refuses in a projected year.
  - Projected rules cite "PROJECTION, NOT PUBLISHED LAW". The corpus version names the year and rates, and `verify` rebuilds it.

No published rule changed, so every 2025–2027 answer is the same as v0.45.0 and the fingerprint is unchanged. core and solve are unchanged; compose is 0.2.0 (new: Form 2210).
