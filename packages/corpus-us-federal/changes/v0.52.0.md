# Corpus v0.52.0

## What's new

- Installment sales (Form 6252) and like-kind exchanges of real property (Form 8824), 2025–2027:
  - **Form 6252, up to four sales** (`installmentSales`, then `installmentSaleN…`), in the year of sale and every later year:
    - `Kind` (`capital`, `section1245`, `section1250`, `other`), `HeldOverOneYear`, `YearOfSale`, the Part I amounts as of the year of sale, `PaymentsThisYear` (line 21) and `PaymentsPriorYears` (line 23), and `ExcludedGain` for a main home.
    - Gross profit, contract price (the mortgage over basis is a line 20 payment) and installment income. Line 19 is exact, or the percentage you used (`GrossProfitPercentageBps`).
    - § 1245 and § 1250 recapture is taxed in full in the year of sale (§ 453(i)); held a year or less, all of a § 1250 property's depreciation is recaptured.
    - A related party's second disposition is a payment (Part III). A related-party sale asks whether § 453(g) applies (`DepreciableToControlledBuyer`: an entity you control or a trust you benefit from, not a family member).
    - The income goes to Schedule D line 4 or 11, or Form 4797 line 4 or 10; unrecaptured § 1250 gain is reported first, and `us.federal.form6252.saleN.unrecaptured_1250_remaining` is what is left.
    - A sale over $150,000 asks `installmentSaleSection453AInterest`; it is never assumed.
  - **Form 8824, up to four exchanges** (`likeKindExchanges`, then `likeKindExchangeN…`):
    - Lines 15–25: cash, other property, liabilities and exchange expenses netted on both sides; realized, recognized and deferred gain; the basis of the property received (25a and 25b by FMV).
    - Line 21 recapture under § 1245(b)(4) and § 1250(d)(4).
    - The recognized gain goes to Schedule D or Form 4797 lines 5 and 16; recognized § 1231 gain on a building is unrecaptured § 1250 gain up to its depreciation.
  - **`compute_return`** shows `forms['6252']` and `forms['8824']`. Form 4797's lines are labeled by the year's form: the 2026 form adds a livestock line 7, so 2026 returns show lines 8, 10, 13 and 20b where 2025 has 7, 9, 12 and 17.
  - **Refused, with the reason:** a sale at a loss or one that is all recapture; a § 453(g) sale; § 453A interest; payments over the contract price; a second disposition with an entered percentage; a related-party exchange followed by a disposition within 2 years; gain recognized on property depreciable to an entity you control (§ 1239), on an exchange or a Form 4797 sale (`businessPropertyNSoldToControlledEntity`); a depreciated capital asset; an interest in a partnership or S corporation.
- `us.federal.estimated.annualized_installment_qN` (the corpus's wage-and-SE annualized method) now refuses when the return has Form 4797, 6252 or 8824 amounts instead of ignoring them. The Form 2210 composer's Schedule AI is unaffected.

Returns without installment sales, like-kind exchanges or business property sales compute exactly as in v0.51.0. A differential on every fixture, the IRS ATS TY2025 scenarios, the AMT regression cases and every pre-existing rule found no difference, and 21,000 random capital gain and AMT evaluations computed identically. The 69 new fixtures reproduce the worked examples in Pub. 537, Pub. 544, the Instructions for Form 8824, Temp. Reg. § 15a.453-1(b)(5) and Reg. § 1.453-12(d). An independent rebuild from the IRS documents agrees on every line of the random returns both compute, and an adversarial review's findings were fixed before release. The fingerprint changes because rules were added and re-versioned (the 32 removed rules are earlier versions of the Form 4797, Schedule D, annualized-installment and Washington rules). compose is 0.8.0 (Schedule AI annualizes the new facts); core and solve are unchanged.
