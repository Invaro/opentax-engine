# Corpus v0.40.0

## What's new

- Turn off defaults. Pass `refuseDefaults: true` to `evaluate` (or `--refuse-defaults` on the command line, `refuseDefaults` on the MCP tools) and every input you didn't give comes back under `NEEDS_FACTS`, with the default it would have used. Accept the ones you agree with in `allowDefaults`. Defaults stay on unless you ask.
- Add your own rules. `extendCorpus` adds your rules alongside the federal ones: add to gross income, adjustments, other taxes or refundable credits with `contributesTo`; read a total computed without your own contribution; or replace a federal rule pinned to its hash, which refuses to load if a later release changes that rule. Your rules share the corpus fingerprint and `verifyProof` checks them as usual.
- Change notes. Every release tag now lists each rule added, removed or changed, and what changed in it, with old and new amounts in dollars.
- License. Releases from October 26, 2026 are under the Business Source License 1.1. See LICENSE and COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md.

The federal and state rules are the same as v0.39.0, so every answer and proof is unchanged.
