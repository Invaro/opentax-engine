# Corpus v0.47.0

## What's new

- Business losses through AGI, 2025–2027 (corpus rules; every return reads them).
  - § 461(l) excess business loss → Schedule 1 line 8p: Schedule C/F results, nonpassive K-1 ordinary business income and loss (new `k1OrdinaryBusinessLoss`), Schedule E when a trade or business. Wages never count. Thresholds $313,000 / $256,000 / $264,000, doubled only for joint returns. The disallowed amount carries forward as an NOL.
  - § 172 NOL deduction → Schedule 1 line 8a: pre-2018 carryovers in full; post-2017 up to 80% of taxable income figured without the NOL deduction and § 199A, with AGI-dependent items figured on AGI before it.
  - Form 172 Part I (`us.federal.nol.generated`) and the § 172(b)(2) carryovers (`us.federal.nol.carryover_pre2018` / `carryover_post2017`), each pool re-run without the § 1211(b) deduction and its own and later NOLs so Social Security and the other AGI items are refigured.
  - AMT: Form 6251 line 2e, and line 2f from `alternativeTaxNolDeduction` (90% limit), asked only with carryovers and AMTI above the exemption.
  - `compute_return` adds a `losses` block in loss years.
  - Refuses, with the reason: business capital gains (Form 4797 isn't modeled), an excess-business-loss NOL deducted alongside QBI, unsettled loss-year items, and the California renter's credit with a federal NOL (it uses California AGI).
- Form 4562 depreciation, 2025–2027: `composeForm4562` in compose 0.3.0, `opentax form4562`, MCP `compute_form_4562`. § 179 with its limits, phase-out and business income carryover; the special allowance by acquisition date (100% after January 19, 2025); MACRS from the printed Pub 946 tables with the mid-quarter test; listed property and the § 280F car limits; Rev. Proc. 87-56 asset classes for cost segregation.

Returns without a large business loss or an NOL compute the same as v0.46.0: a differential on 1,600 random returns found no other difference. The fingerprint changes because rules were added and re-versioned (gross income v17, taxable Social Security v12, AMTI v4–v6, CA renter's credit v2). compose is 0.3.0 (new: Form 4562); core and solve are unchanged.
