# Corpus v0.44.0

## What's new

- Form 6251 line by line. Line 13 is line 4 of the Qualified Dividends and Capital Gain Tax Worksheet, not limited to taxable income, so when net capital gain plus qualified dividends exceeds taxable income the AMT is no longer overstated (MFJ 2025, $10,000 interest and $2,000,000 LTCG: $8,190 → $6,900). Line 40 caps the tentative minimum tax at line 39. Line 1b can be negative. Line 2a follows the deduction actually taken. Line 10 includes the excess advance premium tax credit repayment. The MFS line 4 add-back is applied. 2026 itemizers' AMT income allows the deductions before § 68. A kiddie-tax child gets the full exemption (§ 55(d)(4)(A)(iii) turns off § 59(j)). New targets: `us.federal.amt.amti`, `.exemption`, `.taxable_excess`, `.capital_gain_amount`, `.tentative_minimum_tax`.
- No deductions from a negative AGI. The 7.5% medical floor, the 10% casualty floor and the 60% charitable limit read a negative AGI as zero; the 2026 non-itemizer charitable deduction (§ 170(p)) applies the 60% limit too.
- SALT for married filing separately halves the amount after the phase-down and floor (§ 164(b)(6)(B); Schedule A worksheet line 10). Modified AGI includes the § 911 exclusion.
- Nonrefundable credits are limited by Form 1040 line 18 (`us.federal.tax_before_nonrefundable_credits`), which includes the excess APTC repayment; the repayment moves from `us.federal.other_taxes` to that line.
- The child and dependent care credit reduces earned income by a Schedule C loss. Taxable Social Security counts a Schedule C loss, pre-2019 alimony and taxable HSA distributions. The premium tax credit floors household income at zero and applies Reg. § 1.36B-2(b)(6) below 100% of the poverty line (new fact `ptcEnrollmentEstimateAtLeast100Fpl`, asked only then). Oklahoma's QSS special exemption no longer turns on with a negative AGI.
- `us.federal.balance_due` subtracts estimated payments and the extension payment as well as withholding.

Answers change only where these apply: AMT returns with preferential income above taxable income, a loss year, an ISO exercise, MFS above the add-back threshold, forced itemizing, a qualified disaster loss, a 2026 itemizer above the 37% bracket, or an APTC repayment; returns with a negative AGI; MFS returns with SALT and modified AGI between $250,000 and $350,000; returns with nonrefundable credits and an APTC repayment; CDCC or taxable Social Security with a Schedule C loss; PTC below 100% FPL; and balance due with estimated or extension payments. Every other existing amount is the same. core, compose and solve are unchanged from v0.43.0.
