# Corpus v0.50.0

## What's new

- General business credits, 2025–2027 (Form 3800), with passive activity credits limited by Form 8582-CR first:
  - **Inputs:**
    - `generalBusinessCreditNonpassive` and `specifiedCreditNonpassive` (§ 38(c)(4)(B) specified credits, allowed against the tentative minimum tax).
    - The § 39 carryforwards `generalBusinessCreditCarryforward` / `specifiedCreditCarryforward`.
    - `spouseHasBusinessCredit` (married filing separately).
    - On each passive activity: `Credit`, `PriorUnallowedCredit`, `CreditType` (`general`, `rehabilitation`, `lihc_pre1990`, `lihc_post1989`) and `CreditSpecified`.
  - **Form 8582-CR:**
    - Credits are allowed against the tax on net passive income, then under the special allowances' tax equivalents: $25,000 phased out from $100,000 of modified AGI, from $200,000 for rehabilitation and pre-1990 low-income housing credits, and with no MAGI limit for post-1989 low-income housing credits.
    - Allowed and unallowed amounts are split per activity (Worksheets 5–9); each activity's carryforward is `us.federal.passive_credit.activityN.unallowed`.
  - **Form 3800:**
    - The § 38(c)(1) limit is net income tax over the greater of the TMT or 25% of net regular tax over $25,000 ($12,500 married filing separately with a spouse credit).
    - Specified credits are measured without the TMT.
    - The allowed credit is Schedule 3 line 6a (`us.federal.gbc.allowed`), after every other nonrefundable credit. The unused § 39 amount is `us.federal.gbc.unused`.
  - **`compute_return`** shows line 6a, `forms['8582-CR']` and `forms['3800']`.
  - **Refused:** PTP credits, the empowerment zone credit, net passive income that includes a disposition gain alongside passive credits, and regular tax by another method (e.g. § 911(f) stacking) when Form 8582-CR needs it.
