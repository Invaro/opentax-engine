# Corpus v0.45.0

## What's new

- Tax year 2027. Federal returns compute for 2027 (`--as-of 2027-12-31`) on the published law: Rev. Proc. 2026-35 (brackets, capital gain breakpoints, standard deduction, AMT, EITC, CTC $2,300 / ACTC $1,800, adoption, kiddie tax, QBI threshold and the $415 / $1,030 minimum, student loan, educator $350, § 179, § 461(l), § 448(c), FEIE, qualifying relative, estate and trust brackets), Rev. Proc. 2026-24 (HSA $4,500 / $9,000), Rev. Proc. 2026-26 (premium tax credit percentages) with the 2026 poverty guidelines, SSA's 2027 wage base $192,300 and household threshold $3,100 (91 FR 67380), and SALT at 101% of 2026 ($40,804, threshold $510,050). From 2027 the saver's credit counts only ABLE contributions and the premium tax credit is limited to eligible individuals (OBBBA § 71301). The IRS's 2027 retirement-plan limits and mileage rate are not yet published, so a 2027 IRA, SEP/solo-401(k) or saver's contribution, the retirement maximum targets and the mileage target refuse until they are. States cover 2025 and 2026.
- 2026 fixes: the educator expense amount is $350; gambling losses are 90% of losses limited to winnings (new input `gamblingWinnings`); the 2026 mileage rate is 76¢ from July 1 (new input `businessMilesDrivenFromJuly`).
- The premium tax credit's 400% cliff compares household income to 4 × the poverty line directly (2026 and 2027).
- Additional Medicare Tax no longer counts self-employment net earnings under $400 (§ 1402(b)), in any year.

Answers change for 2025–2026 only where these apply: 2026 educator expenses over $300, 2026 gambling losses, 2026 mileage with miles from July, household income just over 400% of the poverty line, and Additional Medicare Tax with self-employment net earnings under $400. core, compose and solve are unchanged from v0.44.0.
