# Corpus v0.41.0

## What's new

- Form 5695, residential energy credits. Part I, the residential clean energy credit (§ 25D: solar, small wind, geothermal, battery storage, fuel cells), and Part II, the energy efficient home improvement credit (§ 25C: insulation, doors, windows, heat pumps, energy audits), line by line from the 2025 form, each line capped as the form caps it. Both credits ended after 2025, so 2026 costs earn $0; a 2025 clean energy carryforward still applies in 2026. `compute_return` adds Form 1040 line 20, Schedule 3 and the Form 5695 lines.
- Nonrefundable credits now apply in the order the 2025 worksheets give: dependent care, education, saver's, Schedule R, Form 5695 Part II, the child tax credit, adoption, then Form 5695 Part I. Schedule 8812 Credit Limit Worksheet B applies when a Part I or adoption credit is claimed with a qualifying child.
- Capital loss carryovers in and out. Two new inputs, `priorYearShortTermCapitalLossCarryover` and `priorYearLongTermCapitalLossCarryover`, enter last year's carryover in its own bucket before netting. The carryover to next year comes out split, `us.federal.capital_loss_carryover.short_term` and `.long_term`, and `us.federal.capital_loss_carryover` stays their sum. The carryover follows § 1212(b)(2): a loss that couldn't reduce taxable income carries forward in full.
- Qualified Dividends and Capital Gain Tax Worksheet, line 22. The tax on the ordinary part of income comes from the Tax Table when that part is under $100,000, even when taxable income is $100,000 or more, for 2025 and 2026. This can change the tax by a few dollars. `useFormulaMethod` still gives the exact brackets.
- New York (compose 0.1.1). IT-201 line 39 comes from the New York State Tax Table when taxable income is under $65,000. Above $107,650 of New York AGI, line 39 is reported as incomplete, because the tax computation worksheets for that range aren't encoded yet.

core and solve are unchanged from v0.40.0.
