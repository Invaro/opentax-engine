# Corpus v0.42.0

## What's new

- Net rental income in the net investment income tax. A rental's net income now counts toward the 3.8% tax, as Form 8960 line 4a does. A real estate professional's rental that is a trade or business, or a rental grouped with a business the taxpayer materially participates in, stays out with `rentalNotInvestmentIncome`, which is only asked when there is rental income.
- Self-employment tax for both spouses. On a joint return where both spouses are self-employed, each gets their own Schedule SE, with their own $400 floor and their own social security wage base against their own W-2 box 3: `spouseSelfEmploymentNetProfit` and `spouseSocialSecurityWages`, asked only on a joint return. `us.federal.se_tax` and `us.federal.se_tax_half_deduction` add the two; `us.federal.se_tax.taxpayer` and `.spouse` are each Schedule SE. Both profits count in gross income, earned income, QBI and Form 8959. Above the § 199A threshold, two spouses' businesses refuse until per-business QBI ships.
- Retirement contribution maximums. `us.federal.retirement.solo_401k_max.schedule_c` and `.sep_max.schedule_c` follow the IRS Pub 560 Deduction Worksheet for Self-Employed; `.solo_401k_max.s_corp` and `.sep_max.s_corp` start from `ownerEmployeePlanCompensation`. Each solo 401(k) maximum splits into employer, deferral and catch-up parts. The 2025 and 2026 limits come from Notices 2024-80 and 2025-67: § 402(g) (shared with other employers' plans through `otherElectiveDeferrals`), catch-up from 50 and the higher limit at 60 to 63 (`isAge60Through63`), § 415(c), and the § 401(a)(17) compensation cap.

Answers change only where these apply: returns with rental income and MAGI over the NIIT threshold, and joint returns that enter a second spouse's self-employment. Asked on its own, `us.federal.se_tax` or `us.federal.qbi_deduction` now needs the filing status. core, compose and solve are unchanged from v0.41.0.
