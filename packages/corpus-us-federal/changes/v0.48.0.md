# Corpus v0.48.0

## What's new

- California 2026, Form 540: the nine-tier 2026 rate schedules X, Y and Z (FTB's published 2026 schedules), the 2026 Tax Table up to $100,000 built the way FTB builds its printed tables (the schedule at each row's whole-dollar midpoint; this reproduces every cell of the printed 2024 and 2025 tables), and the 2026 amounts from FTB's September 3, 2026 indexing memo: standard deduction $5,900 / $11,800, exemption credits $158 / $491 with the AGI limitation, renter's credit limits $55,830 / $111,660, Young Child Tax Credit $1,229, AMT exemptions and phase-outs, SDI 1.3%.
  - New rules `us.ca.standard_deduction` and `us.ca.exemption_credits` (line 32, the AGI Limitation Worksheet). New facts `caAgi`, `caFederalAgi`, `caPersonalExemptions`, `caDependentExemptions`.
  - The 2026 CalEITC refuses until FTB publishes the 2026 EITC table in the FTB 3514 booklet.
- Five 2025 California fixes:
  - Single and MFS taxable income from $371,479 to $505,208 is taxed at 10.3%, as Schedule X prints; it was taxed at 9.3%.
  - The CalEITC matches all 2,632 cells of the printed 2025 EITC table (775 cells were off, mostly by $1–2), applies the $4,814 investment income limit, and gives $1 on the $32,900 row.
  - Exemption credits are reduced above the AGI thresholds.
  - The renter's credit tests California AGI (Form 540 line 17), not federal AGI, and the joint limit is $107,988.
  - Taxable income of exactly $100,000 uses the Tax Table.
- Calling `us.ca.renters_credit` directly now asks for `caAgi`; `compute_state_return` fills it from line 17.

Nothing outside California changed: every non-California rule gives the same answer as v0.47.0 on every repo fixture, the IRS MeF ATS TY2025 scenarios and the partner-supplied AMT cases, in 2025 and 2026. compose is 0.4.0 (year-aware California return); core and solve are unchanged.
