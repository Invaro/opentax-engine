# Corpus v0.51.0

## What's new

- Sales of business property, 2025–2027 (Form 4797), with unrecaptured § 1250 gain taxed at up to 25%:
  - **Inputs:**
    - Up to eight properties (`businessProperties`, then `businessPropertyN…`): `Kind` (`section1245`, `section1250`, `other`), `HeldOverOneYear`, `SalesPrice`, `CostBasis`, `Depreciation` and, for § 1250 property, `AdditionalDepreciation`. `Qbi` and `NetInvestmentIncome` are asked only when they matter.
    - `section1231LookbackLosses` (line 8), `k1Section1231Gain` / `k1Section1231Loss`, `k1UnrecapturedSection1250Gain`, and `unrecapturedSection1250GainDistributions` (Form 1099-DIV box 2b).
  - **Form 4797:**
    - Each property's gain or loss; § 1245 recapture up to the depreciation, § 1250 recapture up to the additional depreciation.
    - § 1231 netting: a net loss is ordinary; a net gain is ordinary up to the 5-year lookback (§ 1231(c)) and long-term capital gain beyond. Sales within a year are Part II.
    - Line 17 goes to Schedule 1 line 4 and line 9 to Schedule D line 11; `us.federal.form4797.lookback_remaining` is next year's line 8.
  - **The 25% rate:** Schedule D line 19 (`us.federal.schedule_d.unrecaptured_1250`) from the Unrecaptured Section 1250 Gain Worksheet; the regular tax uses the Schedule D Tax Worksheet whenever there is any, and Form 6251 Part III taxes it at 25% (lines 13–15, 35–37).
  - **Where the results go:** AGI and § 86 income; net investment income (Form 8960 lines 5a/5b); QBI for property used in a qualified trade or business; the EITC investment-income test (Pub. 596 Worksheet 1 lines 5–7).
  - **`compute_return`** shows `forms['4797']`.
  - **Refused:** §§ 1252, 1254 and 1255; casualties, installment sales and like-kind exchanges (Forms 4684, 6252, 8824); Part IV; 28% rate gain; a § 461(l) or NOL year with Form 4797 amounts; FEIE stacking or the kiddie tax with Form 4797 amounts; and lookback income alongside QBI property.

Returns without business property sales compute exactly as in v0.50.0. A differential on every fixture, the IRS ATS TY2025 scenarios, the AMT regression cases and every pre-existing rule found no difference, and 3,000 random returns heavy in gains, dividends and AMT preferences computed identically. An independent rebuild of Form 4797, the line 19 worksheet, the Schedule D Tax Worksheet and Form 6251 Part III from the IRS documents agrees on 400 random returns. The fingerprint changes because rules were added and re-versioned (the 32 removed rules are earlier versions of the AMT, EITC, QBI, NIIT, Schedule D, gross income, § 86, kiddie, FEIE, § 461(l) and NOL rules that now read Form 4797). compose is 0.7.0 (Schedule AI annualizes the Form 4797 facts); core and solve are unchanged.
