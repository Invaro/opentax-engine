Fill in the facts (or pick an example) and compute. The answer arrives with its full derivation — every rule cited to the statute or Rev. Proc. it came from — and a proof file you can verify offline. If the encoded law can't answer, it refuses; it never guesses.

Paste a proof artifact (the JSON from “Download proof” here, or opentax eval … --proof out.json on the CLI). Your browser re-derives every step against its own copy of the rule corpus and compares — no server involved.

Computation and citation, not tax advice. The same corpus, hashes and all, runs the CLI: pnpm opentax eval --status mfj --wages 120000 --kids 2 · Apache-2.0
